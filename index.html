Every play on this site goes through a two-input filter. Sweet Spot answers "is this a great HR environment for this hitter, right now?" -- it does not care about price. Dimers answers "what does the market think the chance is?" -- it does not care about quality. The app uses one to validate the other. Sweet Spot is the gate; Dimers is the ranker. Park Factors (stadium + day-of weather) act as a third overlay. Day 67 (Jun 2) is a full 15-game Tuesday card with Great American BP +9% HR as the top HR environment (KC @ CIN), and Target Field / Nationals Park / Busch Stadium all suppressed (-33% HR or worse).

🎯 Sweet Spot QUALITY

How good the HR environment is, built from data that doesn't have a public line:

  • Pitcher Vuln 0–100 (70+ TARGET, 50–69 STRONG, 32–49 MODERATE)
  • Batter ZONE 1–9 ⚡ (premium = 5+)
  • ELITE ISO tier (≥ .250)
  • Park factor + weather, platoon edge, hot/cold streak
  • NEW: Day-of 2B/3B% — drives Doubles board (separate from HR%)

💰 Dimers PRICE

The market's implied probability that a counting stat happens at all:

  • 1+H% — chance of a hit
  • RBI% — chance of an RBI
  • HR% — chance of a HR
  • Ov 0.5 2B% — chance of ≥1 double (Doubles board)
  • Pitcher props — Ks, Outs, ER, Hits allowed
