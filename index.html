Every play on this site goes through a two-input filter. Sweet Spot answers “is this a great HR environment for this hitter, right now?” — it doesn't care about price. Dimers answers “what does the market think the chance is?” — it doesn't care about quality. The app uses one to validate the other. Sweet Spot is the gate; Dimers is the ranker.

The two inputs do different jobs

🎯 Sweet Spot QUALITY

How good the HR environment is for this hitter, built from data that doesn't have a public line:

  • Pitcher Vuln 0–100 (70+ TARGET, 50–69 STRONG, 32–49 MODERATE, <32 TOUGH)
  • Batter ZONE 1–7 ⚡ (premium = 5+)
  • ELITE ISO tier (≥ .250)
  • Park factor + wind, platoon edge, hot/cold streak
  • Tier 3 confirmers: Barrel%, xwOBA, Exit Velo, Adj HR Factor

Output: a tier (0/1/2/3) + ZONE rating + pass/fail on must-haves.

💰 Dimers PRICE

The market's implied probability that a counting stat happens at all, blended from books and projections:

  • 1+H% — chance of a hit
  • RBI% — chance of an RBI
  • HR% — chance of a HR
  • Pitcher props — Ks, Outs, ER, Hits allowed

Output: a single % per prop. Tells you how the books see it; tells you nothing about why.

The formula — a 4-step gate

Run in order. The first three steps are quality. Dimers only enters at step four.

1
SWEET SPOT MUST-HAVES GATE
ZONE ≥ 3⚡ AND ELITE ISO ≥ .250 AND Pitcher Vuln ≥ 50
All three required for Tier 0 / Tier 1. Fail any one → drop to Tier 2/3 or skip pile, regardless of how juicy the Dimers number is.
2
TIER 2 SUPPORT PROMOTE
Any one of: HOT BAT (HR last 7d) · Score ≥ 55 · Park ≥ 1.05 + wind out · Platoon edge + ISO ≥ .180
A near-miss on the gate can climb back if the surrounding context is strong enough.
3
TIER 3 CONFIRMERS STACK
Barrel% ≥ 8% · xwOBA ≥ .330 · Exit Velo ≥ 90 · Adj HR Factor ≥ +10% · CRUSHER badge
Stack these on top of a passing gate to convert a Tier 1 play into a CONVICTION pick.
4
DIMERS PRICE CHECK RANK
Only after the gate passes. Use Dimers HR% / 1+H% / RBI% to rank survivors, not to qualify them.
Sweet Spot decides who plays. Dimers decides who plays first.

TL;DR

Sweet Spot is the gate. Dimers is the ranker.

Pass the gate first → then sort by Dimers % within each tier → then publish to the right board.

When the two disagree and Dimers is hot but Sweet Spot is cold, it goes to the Skip pile, not the play pile.

play = passes_gate(Sweet Spot) × rank_by(Dimers %)
