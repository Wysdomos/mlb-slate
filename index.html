⏱ 30-second version

Every play is graded T0 → T3. Higher is better.

T0 = lock plays, ride them first. T3 = SKIP, leave alone.

Start at the top of any board. The grading already did the sorting for you.

⚖️ Every grade comes from 3 factors

💎

Quality

How good the player is at this stat, right now. Power, K-rate, contact, recent form.

Look for: Score 80+, ZONE ⚡5+, ISO .250+
🆚

Matchup

How vulnerable today's opposing pitcher is. Platoon edges. Hand advantages.

Look for: Vuln 50+, LHB vs RHP or any Switch
🏟

Park

The stadium and today's weather. Some parks turn flyouts into homers. Others kill them.

Look for: HR% +8 or higher (🌋 +25 = volcano)
