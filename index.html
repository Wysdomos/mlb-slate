Every play on this site goes through a two-input filter. Sweet Spot answers "is this a great HR environment for this hitter, right now?" — it doesn't care about price. Dimers answers "what does the market think the chance is?" — it doesn't care about quality. The app uses one to validate the other. Sweet Spot is the gate; Dimers is the ranker. Park Factors (stadium + day-of weather) act as a third overlay. Day 46 (May 12) is a full 15-game Tuesday card with Sutter Health Park +29% HR as the slate volcano (STL@ATH), Rate Field +18% as the secondary HR booster, and Fenway / Truist / PNC all suppressed −20% to −23%. NEW: SP_Projections sheet drives the K board (15 starters), the new SP HR/BB Risk Board, and cross-references into HR / SB / Parlay picks.

🎯 Sweet Spot QUALITY

How good the HR environment is, built from data that doesn't have a public line:

  • Pitcher Vuln 0–100 (70+ TARGET, 50–69 STRONG, 32–49 MODERATE)
  • Batter ZONE 1–9 ⚡ (premium = 5+)
  • ELITE ISO tier (≥ .250)
  • Park factor + weather, platoon edge, hot/cold streak
  • NEW: Day-of 2B/3B% — drives Doubles board (separate from HR%)

💰 Dimers PRICE

The market's implied probability that a counting stat happens at all:

  • 1+H% — chance of a hit
  • RBI% — chance of an RBI
  • HR% — chance of a HR
  • Ov 0.5 2B% — chance of ≥1 double (Doubles board)
  • Pitcher props — Ks, Outs, ER, Hits allowed
1
SWEET SPOT MUST-HAVES GATE
⚡ ZONE ≥ 3 AND ELITE ISO ≥ .250 AND Pitcher Vuln ≥ 50
All three required for T0/T1. Fail any one → T2/3 or skip, regardless of Dimers.
2
PARK FACTOR OVERLAY KEY
HR% from ParkFactors_2026 includes stadium history + day-of weather. ≥+8% boosts; ≤-10% downgrades; ≤-17% skip penalty. 2B/3B% separately governs Doubles board: Fenway +29%, Chase +12%, Oracle +12%, Camden +9% are today's 2B boosters.
For doubles: park 2B/3B% matters — not HR%. A HR-suppressing park (Oracle -16% HR) can still be a 2B gold mine (+12% 2B/3B).
3
TIER 3 CONFIRMERS STACK
Barrel% ≥ 8% · xwOBA ≥ .330 · Exit Velo ≥ 90 · BPP HR% ≥ 20%
4
DIMERS CONFIRM PRICE
Cross-check Dimers HR% and 1+H% to rank within tier. T0 requires Dimers HR ≥ 12% AND BPP HR ≥ 10%. For doubles: Adj Doubles ≥ 0.30 = lock tier.
