Every play on this site goes through a two-input filter. Sweet Spot answers "is this a great HR environment for this hitter, right now?" — it doesn't care about price. Dimers answers "what does the market think the chance is?" — it doesn't care about quality. The app uses one to validate the other. Sweet Spot is the gate; Dimers is the ranker. Park Factors (stadium + day-of weather) act as a third overlay — boosting or penalizing every play based on the actual hitting environment. Starting Day 43 we've added a dedicated Doubles Targets Board: park 2B/3B% (not HR%) drives the doubles projection — Fenway +24% and Chase +10% are the two biggest movers today.

🎯 Sweet Spot QUALITY

How good the HR environment is, built from data that doesn't have a public line:

  • Pitcher Vuln 0–100 (70+ TARGET, 50–69 STRONG, 32–49 MODERATE)
  • Batter ZONE 1–9 ⚡ (premium = 5+)
  • ELITE ISO tier (≥ .250)
  • Park factor + weather, platoon edge, hot/cold streak
  • NEW: Day-of 2B/3B% — drives Doubles board (separate from HR%)

💰 Dimers PRICE

The market's implied probability that a counting stat happens at all:

  • 1+H% — chance of a hit
  • RBI% — chance of an RBI
  • HR% — chance of a HR
  • Ov 0.5 2B% — chance of ≥1 double (Doubles board)
  • Pitcher props — Ks, Outs, ER, Hits allowed
