🧮 HOW IT'S GRADED what counts as a win

HR / Hits / SB / 2B: the bat records 1+. HRR: hits + runs + RBIs ≥ 1. K: strikeouts clear the line (a pitcher's hits, earned runs and outs ride along as context). Totals / NRFI: the lean vs the actual result. Scratches and postponements are voided. Grades pull from the official box score the next morning — no judgment calls. Losses stay on the board.
