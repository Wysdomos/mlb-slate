🎯 What is The Safe K Report?

Every starting pitcher gets graded on a 12-point system — projected strikeouts, how deep they go, walk rate, opposing lineup strikeout tendencies, swing-and-miss rates, and more. The more green boxes a pitcher has, the higher their tier: Diamond down to Borderline.

🔒 Why "Safe"?

The O line we recommend is always the floor — the most conservative bet for that pitcher. A pitcher projecting 6 Ks gets O 3.5, not O 5.5. Even if they underperform a little, you still win. We never chase lines.

➕ How to use these plays

These are designed as extra legs on a parlay — not standalone bets. You already have your main plays locked in. Tack on a Safe K leg to boost the payout without blowing up the slip. Low risk, real value.

📈 Can I go higher than the recommended line?

Always your call. The tier tells you how much cushion you have:

💎 DiamondAll criteria green. Comfortable going up one line for more value.
🏆 EliteVery strong. Stick to recommended or go up one with caution.
💪 StrongSolid play. Stick to the recommended line.
⚪ BorderlineRecommended floor only. Do not go higher.
