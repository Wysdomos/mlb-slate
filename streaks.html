📖 How The Streaks Work

What is a streak? A player who has hit the qualifying stat in back-to-back or consecutive games — not just good recent form, but an active run confirmed by official game logs.

🔥 HRRH+R+RBI ≥ 1 in ≥ 3 consecutive games

🎯 HITAt least 1 hit in ≥ 4 consecutive games
💥 2+HMulti-hit game in ≥ 2 consecutive games
💰 RBIAt least 1 RBI in ≥ 4 consecutive games
🔻 HAPitcher allowed 6+ hits in ≥ 3 consecutive starts (fade)
